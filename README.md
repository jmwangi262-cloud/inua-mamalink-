# Inua MamaLink
### Digital Maternal and Child Nutrition Access Platform

**Developed by:** Inua Slice Foods Ltd  
**Country:** Kenya  
**Status:** Early-stage open-source prototype  
**Technology:** Python, Streamlit, Pandas  
**Licence:** MIT

## About the Project

Inua MamaLink is an open-source digital nutrition supply management prototype developed by Inua Slice Foods Ltd, a Kenyan food processing enterprise working to improve nutrition access, reduce post-harvest losses and support community-based food systems.

The project explores how digital technology and data-driven forecasting can improve access to nutrition supplies for underserved communities, including pregnant women, breastfeeding mothers and young children.

## The Problem

Women and children in rural and low-income communities face barriers to accessing nutritious foods, including fragmented distribution systems, limited visibility of local demand and unreliable supply planning.

Community-based nutrition programmes often depend on manual records, making it difficult to monitor requests, track deliveries and anticipate future requirements.

## Our Solution

Inua MamaLink aims to support nutrition supply coordination through a simple digital platform.

The initial prototype provides:

- Recording of aggregated nutrition supply requests.
- Tracking of requested and delivered quantities.
- Ward-level nutrition supply dashboards.
- Basic demand forecasting using historical records.
- Downloadable CSV reports.
- Open-source code for further development.

## Our Vision

We envision a scalable digital system connecting community nutrition needs with local food producers, processors, distributors and appropriate healthcare partners.

Future development may include automated demand forecasting, secure data management, community health worker coordination and integration with nutrition supply networks.

## Current Development Stage

Inua MamaLink is currently a learning and demonstration prototype using fictional sample data.

It is not yet a validated healthcare solution, does not provide medical diagnoses and has not completed a clinical or community health pilot.

The project is being developed toward real-world testing, responsible data management and improved nutrition supply forecasting.

## Technology

- Python — application programming language.
- Streamlit — web application interface.
- Pandas — data processing and analysis.
- CSV — demonstration data storage and export.
- GitHub — open-source collaboration.

## Getting Started

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Install the packages in `requirements.txt`.
3. Run `streamlit run app.py`.
4. Explore the fictional demonstration data.
5. Test the dashboard and forecasting features.

The app can also be deployed through Streamlit Community Cloud.

## Data Privacy and Safety

This public demonstration uses synthetic data only. Users must not upload names, telephone numbers, addresses, pregnancy information or other sensitive personal and health records.

The prototype is not intended to support clinical decision-making.

## Long-Term Impact

Our goal is to improve nutrition supply planning, reduce distribution inefficiencies, strengthen community access to nutritious foods and develop an adaptable digital tool that can support underserved populations.
